# Presenter view

- **p** pops out the presenter view: keep it on your laptop, put the talk on the projector (**f** for fullscreen).
- It shows the current node at its step, what **Space** does next, the outgoing edges and your speaker notes.
- A timer, the clock and a mini-map of the fabric; click a node on the mini-map to fly there.
- Keys work in either window, so a clicker works whichever one has focus.

::: notes
These lines come from a `::: notes` block. The timer starts with your first move; click it to pause.
:::
