# Citations

- `[@knuth1984]` cites a work from `refs.bib`: [@knuth1984].
- With a page, or several at once: `[@demoura2021, p. 625; @vanwijk2003]` gives [@demoura2021, p. 625; @vanwijk2003].
- Hover a citation for the full reference; click it to fly to the `references` slide, which lists every cited work.

::: notes
The camera's fly transition follows van Wijk and Nuij's smooth zoom [@vanwijk2003].
:::
