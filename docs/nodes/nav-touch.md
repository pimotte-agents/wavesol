# On a phone or tablet

Open a talk on a touch screen and it gets buttons of its own:

- **Swipe** right to left for the next step, left to right to go back.
- **◀ ▶** below the slide do the same; **Next:** shows where the path goes.
- **Detours** lists the other ways on from this slide.
- **▦** opens the overview, **☰** the menu (PDF, fullscreen, gestures).
- In landscape the buttons float in the corners and fade while unused.

::: notes
Nothing to configure: the bars appear whenever the device's main pointer is touch. This is for someone reading the talk on their phone; to control a projector from a phone, pair it (c).
:::
